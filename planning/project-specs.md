### Welcome Committee Monthly Email

I am the volunteer and sole-member of the Welcome Committee for my Catholic Parish.

The role, as it currently stands, is to send a welcome email to every family who signs up to be a member of our parish at the start of the month.

I receive emails containing the new families and their email addresses, names, and other details about them.

I want to automate the process of pulling the information from the emails I receive in my Gmail account, sorting them into the proper buckets for informing other ministry leads of people who are interested in their ministries, and preparing the upcoming month's email using AI to parse the most recent Parish Bulletin, saved down as a PDF from the parish website.

Additionally, automatically update a data file with all scraped email data for review and record-keeping (with privacy safeguards).

I would like assistance planning and implementing the application(s) to perform these tasks.
